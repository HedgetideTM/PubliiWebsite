Frequently Asked Questions:

Is the Hedgetide™ Method difficult and complex?

Using the Hedgetide™ Method is quite simple. HedgeTIDE™ offers its proprietary data with a focus on hedging via options. The brand's methodological modules introduce advanced concepts, such as geometric Brownian motion and the Longstaff-Schwartz algorithm. If you would rather take a deep dive, you are more than welcome to do so. If you, however, are busy in your everyday life, you can glide through. It is not so much the trades that differ, as it is the depth of information.

When can I expect results?

In terms of knowledge and insight, really quickly. Financially, the profit/loss curve of consistent compounded growth tends to begin slowly before it ascends and ultimately skyrockets. We strongly recommend using a simulation account for building investment or trading routines prior to deploying capital.

If you can invest profitably, why do you license the Hedgetide™ Method?

It is a business opportunity that is worth the effort. Investment and trading mentors empower clients. However, that is the reward of doing it, not the reason.

Strategies can be validated through simulation accounts or back-testing. They must be clearly outlined and forgiving. Investment and trading courses should also teach strategy creation.

If the subject matter is this easy to learn, why does the course take this long?

It is quick and easy to learn how to accurately set up multileg structures. However, without the necessary understanding of how the positions offset one another and what the weak points are, maintaining consistency can be a challenge. This is where focused expertise makes all the difference.

What if I am tight on time?

You will not be forced into a rigid bootcamp box. Instead, we give you the steering wheel and the sat nav. If you have a clear runway, you can sprint through the core curriculum in three months. If you need to pace yourself to maintain peak performance at your day job, you have the full six months to execute, or better yet - you can integrate one of our subscription modules seamlessly into your lifestyle.
